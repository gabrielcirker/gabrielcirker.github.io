Research & projects

The current research collection looks at the energy balance of a rowing stroke, the response of beam geometries, and immune activation and regulation.

The immune-dynamics project was created with Kennedy Jamison and Cecilia Pirozzi. It combines ordinary differential equations with custom SINDy equation learning.

Beyond the classroom

Music is a starting point for my Spotify Title Lab and Desktop in Stereo. They give me room to experiment with language, personal data, interfaces, and APIs.

How to read the work

Reports sit alongside source code, figures, and numerical checks. These are computational studies, not experimental or clinical validations. Each project explains its own scope and limitations.

The original mechanics manuscripts list St. John’s University. The revisions separately identify the updated University of Michigan affiliation.
