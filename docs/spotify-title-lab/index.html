Spotify Listening Lab

Music is a big part of my life. I listen pretty much nonstop throughout the day, so I wanted to see what I could do with my own Spotify listening data.

I treated this project as a mental jungle gym: a chance to test my capabilities, reshape the data in different ways, and figure out what I could learn from it.

This page brings together six ways of looking at the data, from how I return to songs to whether my earlier listening helps predict what comes next.
